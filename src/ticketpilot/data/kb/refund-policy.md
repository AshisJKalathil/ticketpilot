# Refund & Return Policy

## Return window
Unused items in original condition can be returned within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund of the item price. The return window is counted from the carrier's delivery date.

## Outside the return window
Items delivered more than 30 days ago are not eligible for a refund. Offer the customer our **1-year warranty** route for manufacturing defects, or store credit at a manager's discretion (escalate to a human for store credit).

## Damaged or defective items
Items that arrive damaged or defective qualify for a **full refund or a free replacement** within **60 days of delivery**. Ask for a photo when the damage is not described clearly. Shipping fees are refunded for damaged items.

## Cancellations
Orders in `processing` status (not yet shipped) can be cancelled for a **full refund**. Shipped orders cannot be cancelled; the customer can return them after delivery.

## Lost packages
If tracking shows a carrier exception such as "lost", or no tracking movement for **7 days**, offer the customer a free reship or a full refund.

## Approval limits
Support agents (human or AI) may issue refunds up to **$500.00** per order. Any refund above $500.00 must be **escalated to a supervisor**. Never refund more than the order total minus amounts already refunded.

## Refund timing
Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5-10 business days.
