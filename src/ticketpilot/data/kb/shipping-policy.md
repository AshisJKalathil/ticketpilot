# Shipping Policy

## Carriers
- **SwiftShip** - standard domestic carrier, 3-5 business days. Tracking available through the SwiftShip API.
- **PacificParcel** - regional carrier for the West Coast, 5-8 business days. PacificParcel has **no API**; tracking is only available on their public web portal.

## Delivery estimates
Orders ship within 1-2 business days. Estimated delivery dates shown on tracking pages are carrier estimates and are not guaranteed.

## Delayed shipments
A shipment is considered delayed when it is more than 2 business days past the carrier estimate. Apologise, share the latest tracking event, and offer to follow up.

## International shipping
We ship to the US and Canada only.
