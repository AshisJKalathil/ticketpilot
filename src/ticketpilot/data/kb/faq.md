# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you offer a warranty?
All gear carries a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects.

## How do I exchange for a different size?
Start a return within 30 days of delivery and place a new order for the size you need. Returns of unused items are free.

## Do you price match?
No, we do not price match.

## How long do refunds take?
Refunds reach the original payment method in 5-10 business days after we process them.

## Can I change my shipping address?
Only while the order is still in `processing` status. Reply with the new address and we will update it.
