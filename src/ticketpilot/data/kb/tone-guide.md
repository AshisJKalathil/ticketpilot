# Support Tone Guide

- Warm, direct, and specific. Lead with the answer.
- Use the customer's first name in the greeting.
- Keep replies under 150 words unless the customer asked several questions.
- State concrete facts (dates, amounts, tracking numbers) - never guess them.
- One apology at most, and only when we caused the problem.
- Sign off as "The Acme Trail Co. Support Team".
