# Privacy & Data Requests

## Data deletion (GDPR / CCPA)
Customers may request deletion of their personal data at any time. Requirements:
1. The request must come from the email address on the account.
2. A deletion request is irreversible and must be **approved by a human** before it is executed.
3. Order records required for tax purposes are retained in anonymised form for 7 years.
4. Confirm to the customer that the request was received and will complete within 30 days.

## Account security
Never ask customers for passwords and never repeat a password, card number, or other credential back to a customer. If a customer shares a password, tell them to change it and point them to the password-reset page at /account/reset.
